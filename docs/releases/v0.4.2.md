A simpler studio: give a task, get a model, fix it in the chat.

**Simpler screen**

- The model takes the whole view. Versions and finished files moved to **History** (the clock button on the right); the stages board and the agent's frames are gone from the screen.
- Under the model: **Download** (GLB, FBX or OBJ — the studio converts with Blender when needed; files are named after the model), **Show folder**, **Model is done** / **Reopen**.
- A done task keeps only its references and download buttons on the right.
- The task panel: name, a plain status and the references on top; how the model is built folds into one line above the chat.
- Tasks and finished models can be deleted (hover the list, or the task's ⋯ menu); they go to the macOS Trash.

**Working with the agent**

- **Mark on the model** right next to the quick replies: click a spot, type what to fix.
- The reviewer's work is visible ("Reviewer: …"), and the card shows how long ago the last action was. After 10 quiet minutes it offers **Restart** — the turn stops and continues where it left off.
- A new task shows an empty view until its first version arrives.

**Reference overlay**

- Lowering the opacity now clears the panel's backdrop too, so the model shows through; the panel resizes up to the whole view, and "rotate the model through the picture" lets you line the model up under it.

**Fixes**

- A second studio window on the same workspace no longer marks a live agent turn as interrupted.
- The download menu no longer closes by itself; generator lines in the chat no longer look like buttons.

macOS, Apple Silicon. The app is not signed: right-click the app → Open → Open on first launch.
