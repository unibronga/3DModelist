Animate a finished model: build a skeleton, bind it, pose it on a timeline — or describe the motion in words and let the agent set the keys.

**Animation tab**

- A finished model now has two tabs on the right: **Model** (view and download, as before) and **Animation**.
- **Build the skeleton by hand:** press on the model and drag to grow a bone, drag from a joint to grow the next one. Joints move from any side; the model turns see-through while you work, the left side is blue and the right side green, and **Mirror** builds both sides at once. Rename or delete bones, undo with ⌘Z. **Human from points** (chin, wrist, elbow, knee, groin) is a quick start that you then edit the same way.
- The app finds the model's left and right by symmetry and turns it to face front; if the character faces away, **Turn around 180°** turns the skeleton with it.
- **Bind to the model:** a windowless Blender computes which bone holds which part — generator meshes cut along seams too. Check it with the **Bend** slider and by clicking a bone to colour what it holds.
- **Motions in packs:** click a bone for rotation rings, release to set a key on the frame. **Move** pulls the bone and its parents along (IK) — the skeleton never comes apart; the hips move the whole body.
- **Timeline** under the model: a resizable card with a row per bone (click a name to select, click twice to rename), smooth / even / jump transitions, mirror pose, copy and paste pose, loop, play with Space.
- **Task for the agent:** a button on a motion opens a chat in place of the timeline. Pick a Claude model and write "make a looping run" or "make the arm swing smoother" — the agent sets the keys on your skeleton, and **Undo the agent's change** brings the previous keys back.
- **Export** a pack or all motions as **GLB** (Godot, web), **FBX** (Unity, Unreal) or **.blend**. Bone names follow Unity Humanoid when built from points; looping motions get a `-loop` suffix so Godot loops them. The model itself is never changed: everything lives in `anim/<model>/` of the workspace.

**Everywhere**

- **Undo / Redo** buttons at the start of the view toolbar, and ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z now work (the Edit menu no longer swallows them); in text fields they edit text as usual.
- **Orientation cube** in the top-right corner of the view, with axes named as in Blender: click a face, edge, corner or axis to snap the view, drag to orbit. Light in the light theme, dark in the dark one.
- **Tooltips on every button**: name, shortcut and what the button does, in all seven languages.
- **One selection on the left:** either a task or a finished model is selected, and the right side always shows that one.

**Fixes**

- A task no longer picks up newer models from other tasks' folders.
- The view no longer freezes if a per-frame overlay fails.

macOS, Apple Silicon. The app is not signed: right-click the app → Open → Open on first launch.
