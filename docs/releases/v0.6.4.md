Generator models now arrive in meters and facing front; the task history moves into the right panel; settings get a section menu.

**Generators**

- **Models in meters, facing front, on the floor.** Generators return the figure in their own units and sideways — a Tripo P2 character showed up 100 m tall in the view. Right after download a windowless Blender now turns it to face front, scales it to the height you enter and stands it on the floor. The fitted GLB sits next to the raw file and opens first; the raw file stays untouched.
- **Model height** field in the generator form: 1.70 m by default — enter the character's height or the object's height.
- Without Blender the raw file is shown as before.

**Task panel**

- **History** is now at the bottom of the task's right panel, under Download: a list of versions with thumbnails, newest first. Its frame fills the panel down to the bottom and the list scrolls inside it. While the agent works, History is a collapsed block under the build options, so the chat keeps its room.
- The fitted generator model is labeled **Generator model in meters**, the raw one stays **Raw generator model**.

**View**

- **Center** button on the tool rail and the **C** key: bring the model back to the middle of the view without changing the angle (Home still also resets to the 3/4 view).

**Settings**

- A section menu on the left — Interface, Workspace, Claude, Generators, Blender — and one section on the right instead of one long page. A section that blocks work gets a red dot, and the window opens on it.

macOS, Apple Silicon. The app is not signed: right-click the app → Open → Open on first launch.
