Works with 3DPainter both ways, skins models made of many pieces without seams, and the motion agent now looks at its own work from every side. Covers 0.7.0–0.8.4.

**3DPainter**

- **File ▸ Open a model from 3DPainter** (or drop a `.3dpaint` on the window): the layers are merged exactly as 3DPainter exports them, and the painted model lands in finished models. A painted face becomes its own **Face** material with the neutral expression; the expression atlases and their description ride along in every download.
- **Zones** (named sets of faces in 3DPainter 1.3.0+) arrive as their own materials named after the zone, listed in `<name>_face.json`.
- **File ▸ Save the model to 3DPainter**: the same project gets the skeleton and motions, face for face; layers, face, zones and paint variants stay byte for byte, so 3DPainter opens it for more painting.
- The project carries the skeleton and motions on its own: opened on another computer or under another file name, they are restored with keys and easing.
- The mesh keeps its name in every GLB the app writes (it used to arrive in Blender as `Mesh_0`), and splitting off the face no longer multiplies vertices.

**Animation**

- **Skinning without seams** on generator models made of dozens of pieces (jacket, shirt, belt): weights come from one solid body shell and are smoothed across parts.
- **Mitten fingers**: one bone per hand for all four fingers plus the thumb.
- The motion agent checks each motion on a front, side and back sheet with measurements and fixes it up to two rounds; "Human by points" no longer swaps left and right.

**Improve a finished model**

- A block in the right panel: Check, Normals, Repair, Clean up, Fewer faces, Retopology, Shading. Each step runs in a windowless Blender on a copy; Undo step, Back to original, and **Save as a new model** — the original is never changed.
- Repair fills only small holes; open edges of clothing are reported, not closed.

macOS, Apple Silicon. The app is not signed: right-click the app → Open → Open on first launch.
